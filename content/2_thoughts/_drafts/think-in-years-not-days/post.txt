Title: Think in years, not days

----

Description: 

----

Date: 2026-02-01

----

Text:

Things are moving fast. So fast, that it feels the progress we made in technology over the last 40 years, is something we'd be able to achieve in the next 4.

Staying relevant is demanding. Staying in the know is tedious.

I envy the simpler times*. When projects used to take years to finish, and come out so beautifully that people for the next 100 years would study it, talk about it, adore it.

- The Taj Mahal
