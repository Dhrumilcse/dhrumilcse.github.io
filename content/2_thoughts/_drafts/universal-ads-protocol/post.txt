### What it is
Agentic Ads. 

>Promote Acme Cloud Runner in Canada for one week. Spend at most CAD 500. Pay no more than CAD 1.25 per click. Show it only in running or fitness contexts.

The protocol gives Instacart Ads a second front door for machines. The existing Ads Manager remains the human front door. Both ultimately call the same Instacart campaign services.

#### What should v1 look like?
Version one should support only:
- One verified advertiser domain
- One product or destination URL
- One traffic objective
- Native headline, body, image, and CTA
- Contextual topics and country targeting
- CPC pricing
- Total and daily spend ceilings
- Start and end times
- Aggregate impressions, valid clicks, and spend
- Mandatory “Sponsored” disclosure
- Platform-native rendering and moderation

Explicitly exclude initially:
- User identifiers
- Customer-list uploads
- Retargeting
- Conversion attribution
- Real-time auctions
- Video
- Arbitrary HTML or JavaScript
- Automated cross-platform budget optimization
- Payment settlement
