Text:

How deep you should be rooted with your identity? your lineage? your history?

I have two contradictory thoughts. One, it is an artificial concept. There's a famous bollywood movie dialouge, "naam me kya rakha hai?" which translates to "What's in the name?", and people should not care about the names, specifically last or family names

On the other hand, it connects you with your roots. Your heritage? Give your persona a character. A context. And without context, it is kind of hard to understand pretty much anything. 

How names came into existance?
What was the first ever family name? 
When passports became a thing?


People needed the family name and signet rings and all the other fun stuff before the world went digital, to trade, to decide whether or not to trust something. But in this day and age, do we need it?
