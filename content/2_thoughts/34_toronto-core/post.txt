Text:

After five years in Toronto, I've started developing my own idea of what constitutes the city's core. It's ever-evolving and in no particular order:

1. Pre-order a pie at Badiali's
2. Gelato from Ed's Real Scoop
3. Catching a Jays game when the dome is open
4. Coffee Crisp
5. Sunset at Riverdale Park
6. Croissant from Brodflour
8. Jelly King and Tacos on Ossington (including the 45-minute wait)
9. Complain about traffic on the Gardiner, while stuck on the Gardiner
10. A summer swim at Woodbine
11. Drake
12. A classic movie at Revue Cinema, with popcorn
13. Missing a streetcar because it just went out of service
14. Humber Bay Arch Bridge
15. Hot chocolate from Eataly on Christmas Eve at the Distillery District 
16. A cheap concert at Budweiser Stage or Danforth Music Hall
17. Watching raccoons climb a tree or eat garbage
18. Highland cattle at High Park Zoo
19. Panko Tofu Bao from Banh Mi Boys
20. Grab a pastry and walk around Kensington Market
21. Loga's Corner
