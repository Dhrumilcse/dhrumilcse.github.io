A while ago, (link: https://farza.com/ text: Farza) created and shared (link: https://freewrite.io/ text: Freewrite macOS) app for distraction free writing in your flow state. I, a sucker for minimal apps, immediately downloaded the app and loved using it. 

After a while, I realized most of my thoughts or reflection occur when I am away from the screen. While walking, camping with friends, driving, running an errand, you name it. While it's not always possible to carry macbook, the phone is always there. Thus, I decided to write (or ask claude to write) the iOS version of the app.
