Title: Life moves in phases

----

Description: 

----

Date: 2024-11-14

----

Text:

There are going to be days when you feel like you’re not accomplishing anything. And that’s okay. There are going to be weeks when you feel you’re going to concur the world. But the very next, you might feel you’re somewhere beneath the ocean. And that’s okay.

No matter how long each one of those lasts, your goals should not change. One should accept that progress happens in phases. 

Life. Life moves in phases. And that’s more than okay.
